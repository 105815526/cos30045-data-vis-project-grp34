Hospitalised Injuries from Road Crashes in Australia (2011–2021)

Project By Dylan Ting Shu Han 105815322 Stanley Sia Ming Qian 105815526 Lee Kok Kiong 104399870

Road crashes put a heavy, ongoing strain on Australia's healthcare system. While fatal crashes usually get the most news coverage, non-fatal injuries actually lead to far more hospital admissions, long inpatient stays, and ongoing recovery care.

Using official 2011–2021 data from the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE), this interactive dashboard makes the numbers easier to explore. Our charts point out major reporting changes in Victoria (2012) and New South Wales (2017), compare actual case increases against percentage shifts for different road users, and look at hospital bed days and regional differences to help guide road safety and healthcare planning.
