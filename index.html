Hospitalised Injuries from Road Crashes in Australia

Road traffic crashes impose a significant burden on the Australian healthcare system. While fatalities often dominate public attention, the non-fatal injury burden represents a substantial and ongoing public health challenge resulting in prolonged hospital stays and long-term rehabilitation. This dashboard visualises data from 2011 to 2021 to support evidence-based targeted safety campaigns and medical resource allocation.
